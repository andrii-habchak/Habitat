## Defaults taken

1. `Weekday` raw values are ISO (Monday = 1 ... Sunday = 7), also in the export JSON
   (`"weekdays": [1, 7]`). Foundation's `weekday` numbering (Sunday = 1) is not used.
2. Export JSON shapes: `DayKey` as `"yyyy-MM-dd"`; goal as `{"kind":"daily"}` /
   `{"kind":"timesPerWeek","count":N}`; reminder kind as `{"type":"general","label":…}` /
   `{"type":"habit","habitID":…}`; time as `{"hour":H,"minute":M}`; weekdays sorted; UUIDs in
   Foundation's uppercase form. No `exportedAt` or app-version field (not in the spec).
3. General reminders have no target, so two general reminders at the same time with an
   overlapping weekday clash even when their labels differ. An edit is not compared with its
   own old version (same id).
4. A general reminder needs a non-blank label; every reminder needs at least one weekday.
5. Clash message format: `Clashes with the reminder "Water" at 08:00 on Mon, Wed.` /
   `Clashes with the reminder for "Read" at …` (English; localisation is not in scope).
6. Archived habit: active through its archive day (inclusive). Its streak is frozen at its
   value on the archive day; completions after the archive day are ignored.
7. Streaks start no earlier than the created day; the creation week of a weekly habit counts
   like any other week (met if c ≥ N even when created mid-week). Weekly status itself does
   not special-case the creation week (PLAN §2 formula as written: a past creation week with
   c < N is `failed`).
8. Weekly streak: an unmet current week (in progress or already unreachable) is skipped, not
   a break; the streak then counts back from the previous week.
9. `daysLeft` (`r`) is 7 for a week that has not started yet.
10. Planner: today's reminders at or before the current minute are skipped (strictly later
    only); habit reminders are skipped for a missing habit and on days the habit is not active
    (before created, after archived). Order: day, time, reminder id; the cap keeps the
    earliest 60.
11. Habit colour is stored as `#RRGGBB` text (validated); the palette is P3's choice.
12. Import rejects (rather than repairs) duplicate habit/reminder ids, duplicate completions,
    completions or reminders pointing at a habit not in the file, invalid habits, and
    reminders that clash with an earlier reminder in the file. `schema` must be the integer 1.
13. `Month.grid` uses as many whole weeks as needed (4 to 6 rows), not always 6.
14. Supported years 0001–9999 (four-digit `yyyy`).
15. A few parameterised tests use 3- and 4-element tuples; those lines carry
    `swiftlint:disable:next large_tuple`.
